## What you're getting at v1.0

DEUS is the operator's CRM. Built around five phases — survey,
blueprint, build, operate, scale — so a real revenue engine sits where
spreadsheets and three half-installed tools used to be.

This release is the public 1.0. Six months of building, four weeks of
hardening, one bank-grade and GDPR-grade security baseline that every
shipped commit had to pass.

Everything below works on day one.

### Pipeline that actually runs

- **Custom pipelines per industry.** Real estate, hospitality, energy,
  and adjacent ops each get a starting blueprint. Rebuild stages,
  rename phases, drop fields you don't want.
- **Deals with money you can trust.** Currency, expected close, win
  probability. Aggregations on the dashboard tell you what's at the
  top, what's slipping, what closes this quarter.
- **Contacts and companies, not "leads".** The same person can be a
  prospect, a partner, an investor, and a referral source — DEUS
  doesn't force a single label. Tag, scope, sort.

### Calendar that surfaces context where you need it

- **Connect Google Calendar or Outlook / Microsoft 365** in two clicks.
  Read-only, scope-minimised, your tokens encrypted at rest.
- **Real-time push-sync.** When a meeting moves on your calendar, DEUS
  knows in seconds — not the next time you reload.
- **Event context on every deal.** The next conversation with the
  prospect appears next to the deal it belongs to. No copy-paste.

### Billing built for trust

- **14-day free trial, no card up front.** Start, evaluate, decide.
- **Self-service plan changes via Stripe Customer Portal.** Upgrade,
  downgrade, swap card, see invoices. We don't sit between you and
  your billing.
- **EU VAT collection** on every invoice — handled cleanly by Stripe,
  reverse-charge mechanics where they apply.

### Team and seats, the way you'd want them

- **Invite by email.** Single-use token, expires in seven days, signs
  the new teammate into the right organisation with the right role.
- **Seat counter on the dashboard.** Used vs. limit, pending invites
  visible. Going past your limit blocks the next invite — no surprise
  bill at month-end.
- **Roles that mean something.** Admin (everything), manager (no
  billing or destructive ops), viewer (read-only).

### Privacy, on the record

DEUS is built under EU law. The boring details, made simple:

- **Your data is yours.** Export it any time from Settings → Privacy.
  JSON archive, structured, downloadable in a click. Includes your
  contacts, deals, notes, calendar metadata, audit history — minus
  the credentials we deliberately keep encrypted.
- **Right to erasure works.** Delete your account and we soft-delete
  for thirty days, then everything goes. No hidden archive.
- **Cookieless analytics.** Plausible, EU-hosted, page counts only.
  No personal identifier, no cross-site tracking, no ad network. Opt
  out from `/privacy` if you'd rather not even be counted.
- **Audit log on every privileged action.** Who connected the
  calendar, who clicked checkout, who changed the seat count. Visible
  to admins. Pruned on a schedule.

### Operator-grade plumbing under the hood

You won't see most of this. It's the reason the rest holds up.

- **AES-256-GCM encryption** for every OAuth token, every webhook
  secret, every credential at rest.
- **Timing-safe HMAC** on every webhook delivery from Google, Stripe,
  and Microsoft. Replay-protected at the edge.
- **Rate limits on every mutation route.** A buggy client or a
  misbehaved automation can't thrash your data.
- **Bank-grade security baseline** — `requireRole` on mutations,
  organisation-scoped queries, atomic transactions on critical writes,
  generic 500s, no PII in logs.

## What's next on the roadmap

Honest about what's not here yet:

### v1.1 — calendar two-way sync
Today DEUS reads your calendar. v1.1 will write back: create a
follow-up directly on your calendar from a deal, and have the
external attendees notified through your provider's normal channel.
Targeting late May.

### v1.2 — AI attributes on contacts
Industry tags, ICP fit scores, summary cards — auto-populated by an
LLM with a clear "edit this" path. You control the prompt; we ship a
sensible default. Targeting June.

### v1.3 — SWR rollout for instant navigation
The dashboard already feels fast. v1.3 makes navigation between pages
feel free — list queries cached and revalidated in the background so
the next click is instant. Targeting June.

### Beyond v1
- Multi-calendar selection (work + personal in the same view)
- Per-tenant database deployment for clients with isolation
  contracts (already specified, ships when the first contract asks)
- Custom AI workflows on contacts (Attio-style, but yours)
