## Why this works (internal — not in email)

- **One sentence reason**: not 3 paragraphs of apology. Customer
  respects competence + brevity more than effusive apology.
- **"Geen drama" / "No drama"**: pre-empts the customer's natural
  "is iets stuk?" panic. Tone-setting before they assume worst.
- **Bullet list "Nothing else changes"**: reduces uncertainty
  about what *did* change. Frame: schedule shifted, contract didn't.
- **No phone-call follow-up needed**: this email is self-contained.
  Customer probably won't reply except to acknowledge.

## What NOT to do

- Don't reschedule via SMS instead — email gives written record.
- Don't apologise more than once.
- Don't explain what was broken in technical detail.
- Don't promise it'll never happen again. Just deliver Wednesday.

## Follow-up if customer pushes back

If customer replies "can you guarantee Wednesday?":

> "Yes. Tomorrow is verification + retest. Wednesday morning I do
> a 1-hour smoke check at 08:00 before your 09:00 start. If
> anything fails that check, I call you direct before 08:30."

Short, specific, no hedging.
