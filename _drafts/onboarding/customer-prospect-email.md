## 3 · Day-one welcome email (sent on invite acceptance)

Subject: **Welcome to DEUS — read this before you click anything else**

Welcome, [first name].

You're in. Three things before you do anything else.

**1. Pick your industry.** Top-right of the dashboard. We seed a
pipeline for it. You can rebuild every stage afterwards — the
default just gets you off zero.

**2. Connect your calendar.** Settings → Integrations → Connect
Google Calendar (or Outlook). Two clicks, read-only scope, your
tokens encrypted at rest. Meetings on a deal will start showing up
next to the deal automatically.

**3. Drop one real contact and one real deal.** Don't seed a fake
test account — DEUS is built for the operator who already has a
pipeline; the first thing it should do is show you yours. Open
**Contacts → New**, paste a real prospect, then **Deals → New** and
attach them.

That's it. The dashboard will start telling you something the moment
the data is real.

**If anything trips:**
- support@lucen.ai — real human, EU hours, response within one
  working day.
- The privacy page (`/privacy`) shows you exactly what we store and
  how to export or delete it.
- Settings → Billing — Stripe Customer Portal, your invoices, your
  payment method, your cancellation if you ever want it.

I'm on the other end. We talked at signup; nothing about that
changed.

— Juan
LucenAI / DEUS
