## What we don't charge for

- Per-seat fees (your stagiair gets a login)
- API requests
- AI usage (we absorb LLM cost into the flat rate)
- Storage (within reason — see below)
- Custom branding
- Audit-log retention
- Number of contacts, deals, or projects

---

## What's "within reason"

We're a small team running honest numbers. If you push 2 TB of
attachments at us or send 50k emails a day from your account, we'll
have a conversation. So far this has never happened, but we say it
out loud so there are no surprises.

## FAQ

**Is this really the price forever?**
Yes, for the first 5 customers, in writing. We'll send you a side
letter confirming the lock when you sign up.

**Why so cheap?**
We need 5 customers using DEUS daily to find the rough edges.
You're paying us for the product; we're paying you in lifetime price.

**Does the price include VAT?**
No. VAT is added at your country's rate. EU B2B with valid VAT ID
is reverse-charge.

**Payment methods?**
SEPA bank transfer (default for EU customers) or Stripe card.
Invoices payable within 14 days. We're happy to invoice annually
if you prefer (no discount for annual).

**Where is my data stored?**
Servers in Falkenstein, Germany. Backups in Amsterdam. Data never
leaves the EEA. Read the [DPA](/legal/dpa).

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes, with 30 days' notice. Export everything as JSON before you
go. We don't lock you in technically; we just hope you stay.

**What happens after my 12-month feedback commitment?**
You stay at €99/mo. The feedback calls become optional. If we ask
for a quote for a case study and you say no, we're fine with that.

**Can I switch verticals later?**
Both verticals are active from day one. There's no switching.

## Why this works (internal note, not on the public page)

- **€99 flat is honest about phase** — we're not enterprise-ready
  yet, the price reflects that, customers don't feel ripped off
  when something is missing.
- **Lifetime lock for 5 customers ≈ €15k missed revenue over 5
  years** — that's the cost of credibility and 5 reference customers.
- **€1.500 migration fee separates serious customers from
  tire-kickers** — anyone willing to pay €1.500 cash up front is
  going to use the product.
- **Beta cohort closes hard at customer #6.** No exceptions. The
  scarcity is real; pretending otherwise destroys the lock-in
  promise for #1-5.
- **Recurring MRR at 5 × €99 = €495 covers DEUS infra (€120/mo) +
  Stripe fees** — the business is self-funding from day one. Juan
  is not, and that needs separate runway planning (see
  /runway-calc output 2026-05-08).
