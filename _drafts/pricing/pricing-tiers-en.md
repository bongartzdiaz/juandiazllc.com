## How to pick a tier

**Starter (€40 / seat)** — small teams trying DEUS as their first
CRM, or migrating from a spreadsheet. You get the full Core CRM,
calendar sync, basic email, and 30-day audit log. No AI yet, no
push sync, no advanced integrations.

**Professional (€69 / seat)** — most mid-market teams land here.
You get AI lead scoring + contact attributes, real-time calendar
push sync, custom SMTP, webhooks, and full API access. This is
the default we recommend.

**Business (€99 / seat)** — for teams with security or compliance
requirements: SSO, IP allowlist, unlimited audit log, 99.5% uptime
SLA, private Slack channel for support. Also unlocks the deeper AI
features (deal summaries, task suggestions).

**Enterprise (Contact us)** — when "shared multi-tenant" is not
enough: dedicated server, custom DPA, country-of-choice data
residency, white-label, custom integrations, phone support, and
99.95% SLA. Pricing is per organisation, not per seat.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why a €40 floor — why not €20 like HubSpot Starter?**
HubSpot Starter is a loss leader feeding into their €1,000/month
Marketing Hub. We don't have that funnel. €40 is the lowest price
at which we can deliver on the GDPR, EU-data, and audit-log promises
without sacrificing infrastructure.

**Why a minimum seat count?**
Solo freelancers aren't our target. Teams of 3+ get more out of
DEUS than individuals. Minimum seats also let us run a smaller
support load while still giving every customer real attention.

**Can I switch tiers anytime?**
Yes. Upgrade is immediate and prorated. Downgrade takes effect at
the next billing cycle. You don't lose data on downgrade — features
become read-only or disabled until you upgrade again.

**What happens if I go over a limit?**
You get a notification at 80% of any limit. We'll never block your
workflow without warning. If you stay over the limit consistently,
we'll discuss the right tier — usually it means upgrading.

**Is the AI add-on or included?**
Included. From Professional upwards, AI lead scoring and contact
attributes are built in. We absorb the LLM cost into the tier
price rather than charging separately.

**What's the difference between Business and Enterprise SSO?**
Business gets SAML 2.0 SSO on our standard provider list. Enterprise
gets custom SSO with your specific identity provider, including
SCIM provisioning and just-in-time user creation.

**Do you support multi-currency?**
Prices shown are EUR ex-VAT. We support EUR, USD, and GBP at
checkout; the price is identical (we don't currency-convert at a
discount or premium). Invoices are issued in your chosen currency.

**Can I get a custom plan between Professional and Business?**
No. Tiers are fixed to keep the product simple. Either you need
the Business features or you don't. We won't bend prices because
it makes the customer experience inconsistent.

**Where is my data stored?**
Hetzner Falkenstein (Germany) for production, Backblaze B2 Amsterdam
for backups. Data never leaves the European Economic Area. Enterprise
customers can choose a specific EU country.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes. Monthly customers cancel any time with 30 days' notice. Annual
customers can request a refund for unused months within the first
30 days. After that, the annual term completes — but you can still
export all data via Settings → Privacy → Export.

## Internal notes (not on public page)

Why this structure beats prior drafts:

1. **Per-seat scales with customer success** — a 5-person team
   pays €345/month on Pro; a 25-person team pays €1,725/month. The
   relationship between value delivered and price paid is
   transparent.
2. **€40 floor positions premium without being expensive** — above
   Pipedrive bottom (€14) and HubSpot Starter (€20), at peer to
   Attio Plus (€34) and Close Startup (€59). We don't compete on
   price; we compete on EU-by-default.
3. **Min-seats per tier filters prospects** — solo founders looking
   for a €20/month CRM bounce at Starter min-3 (€120 floor). Mid-
   market teams of 5-15 self-qualify for Pro or Business. This
   replaces a sales team.
4. **AI in Pro upwards (not Starter, not add-on)** — Starter buyers
   are price-sensitive and don't expect AI. Pro buyers expect AI
   bundled. Add-on pricing would fragment the page.
5. **Business tier exists to catch mid-market that would otherwise
   churn to HubSpot/Close** — €99 is below HubSpot Pro €100 and
   Close Pro €109, with materially better GDPR posture. Lose this
   tier and you push 10-25-seat customers to competitors.
6. **Beta cohort is legacy clause, not active sales tool** — once
   filled (or if it never filled), the clause sits in archive. No
   ongoing "5 spots, 3 left" urgency-marketing, which becomes
   stale if not maintained.
