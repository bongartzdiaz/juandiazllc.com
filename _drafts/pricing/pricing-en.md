## FAQ

**Does the price include VAT?**
No. VAT is added at the rate that applies to your country. EU B2B with a valid VAT ID is reverse-charge.

**What payment methods do you accept?**
SEPA bank transfer (default for EU customers) and Stripe (card). Invoices are payable within 14 days.

**Can I switch verticals later?**
Yes. Contact support — we activate the second vertical the same day. No data is migrated; you get fresh modules for the new vertical.

**Where is my data stored?**
On servers in Falkenstein, Germany. Backups in Amsterdam. We do not transfer data outside the EEA. Read the [DPA](/legal/dpa).

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes, with 30 days' notice. We don't lock you in. You can export everything as JSON before you leave.
