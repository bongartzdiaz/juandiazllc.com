# Pitch template — operator field reports

How to pitch a piece to industry trade publications without sounding
like every other founder pushing thought leadership. The angle is
**field report, not pitch deck**: I'm the operator, here's what I
learned building X, here's the data from N installations / deals /
properties, here's what surprised me, here's what didn't work.

This template is referenced from `SEO.md` (Tier 1 backlink targets:
Solar Magazine NL, PV Magazine DE, Tweakers, El Confidencial). Use it
to draft outreach to editorial leads.

---

## Subject line

Pick one of these patterns. Keep it under 70 chars.

- `Field report: [N installations / X deals / Y properties] — [counter-intuitive finding]`
- `[Vertical] data — [N data-points] from [time-window]`
- `What I learned building [product] — [N customers]`

**Examples that work:**

- "Field report: 84 saldering-2027 calculator runs — owners who don't have batteries break even faster"
- "Real estate ops data — 312 lead-to-call conversions, 94% of leakage was in two specific stages"
- "What I learned building Voltafy — 47 commercial PV installs, the spreadsheet error that costs 11% of yield"

**Don't use:** "I'd love to write for you", "thought leadership",
"insights you might find interesting", anything that sounds like a
PR pitch.

---

## Body (≤ 200 words)

```
Hi [editor first name],

I run [company] — we [one-sentence what we actually do, not
positioning fluff]. Last [quarter / six months / year] we [shipped
N things / handled N customers / processed N transactions], which
means I'm sitting on [specific dataset].

I'd like to write a piece for [publication] called "[working title]"
— [one sentence on the angle, what's counter-intuitive about it,
what the reader walks away with].

The piece would include:
- [3 specific data points the reader will see]
- [1 chart or table you'd build from your own data]
- [1 thing that didn't work and why]

I'm not pitching a product. I'd disclose [company] as the source of
the data and link to my work page at the bottom of the piece — no
inline product mentions.

Word count target: [800-1200]. I can have a draft ready in [N days].

Best,
Juan Diaz
[link to /about]
[link to one prior published piece if available, else /insights]
```

---

## Body — what NOT to write

- "I think your readers would find this valuable." → They wouldn't,
  the editor decides what their readers find valuable. Just describe
  the piece.
- "I have unique insights into..." → Everyone says this. Show, don't
  claim.
- "This would be a great fit for your publication." → Editor knows
  better than you what fits. Just pitch the piece.
- Any superlatives ("revolutionary", "groundbreaking",
  "game-changing"). Bin.

## Per-publication cheat sheet

### Solar Magazine NL (DA ~55)

- **Editor**: check masthead at solarmagazine.nl/over-ons
- **Language**: Dutch preferred, English accepted for technical pieces
- **Angle**: regulatory + market — saldering 2027, btw, energiebelasting,
  netcongestie. Less about residential install tips, more about the
  policy + ops side.
- **Word count**: 800-1500 typical
- **What works**: "what changed this quarter and what installers
  should do about it"

### PV Magazine DE (DA ~65)

- **Editor**: check masthead at pv-magazine.de/impressum
- **Language**: German strongly preferred. English accepted for
  international features but pieces by international authors usually
  get translated by their team.
- **Angle**: Solarpaket I, BImSchG changes, EEG amendments, grid
  capacity. Operator angle on regulation is rare and welcome.
- **Word count**: 1200-2000 typical
- **What works**: clear distinction between residential / commercial /
  utility-scale; their readers are sharp and notice when you don't.

### Tweakers.net (DA ~80)

- **Editor**: redactie@tweakers.net
- **Language**: Dutch only
- **Angle**: AI + energy tech, automation in operator workflows, the
  build side (you actually wrote code, not "we used AI to..."). Their
  audience is engineers — they smell PR from a mile away.
- **Word count**: 600-1500 — they prefer tighter
- **What works**: "I built X, here's the architecture, here's what
  I'd do differently next time". Vendor-neutral.

### El Confidencial (tech section — DA ~80)

- **Editor**: tecnologia@elconfidencial.com
- **Language**: Spanish. Argentinian / Castilian both accepted.
- **Angle**: autoconsumo, aerotermia, nuevo Real Decreto for
  residential / commercial PV, Iberian electricity market quirks.
- **Word count**: 1000-1800 typical
- **What works**: data-driven pieces with concrete numbers from real
  Spanish installations. They get a lot of generic "energy transition"
  pitches and prefer specificity.

## Follow-up

If no reply in 7 calendar days, send one follow-up. After that, drop
it — re-pitching the same publication on a different angle 2 months
later is fine, repeating the same pitch isn't.

Follow-up template:

```
Hi [name],

Following up on the field-report pitch from [date]. Happy to skip
this one if it's not a fit — just want to confirm it landed.

Best,
Juan
```
